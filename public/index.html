    Analytics & KPI Guide

    Comprehensive guide to metrics, calculations, and display locations across OmniPrice

    Home Page Overview KPIs

    6 Metrics · Products Tab
    Tracked Items Counter showTrackedKpi

    Displays the total scale of your monitored catalog on the home page overview banner, including how many products are currently active and how many are paused.

    Calculation: Total count of unarchived products in database, partitioned into active vs. paused states.
    Record Lows (All-Time Lows) Counter showRecordLowsKpi

    Identifies how many products in your catalog are currently priced at or below their all-time lowest recorded price since tracking started.

    Calculation: Evaluates current lowest store price against historical minimum (lowestPrice ≤ minPriceEver).
    Total Savings Counter showTotalSavingsKpi

    Quantifies the cumulative financial discount across all tracked products that are currently discounted below manufacturer MSRP / reference price.

    Calculation: Sum of (referencePrice - lowestPrice) across all discounted products, paired with the active deals count.
    Store Feeds Health Counter showStoreFeedsKpi

    Monitors scraper infrastructure reliability. Shows what percentage of configured merchant store sources are actively returning valid price extractions.

    Calculation: (Healthy price-bearing sources / Total active sources) × 100%. Displays warning state if feeds drop below 90%.
    Average Catalog Discount Counter showAvgDiscountKpi

    Measures promotional depth across your entire catalog. Shows whether merchants are offering aggressive markdowns or holding prices close to MSRP.

    Calculation: Mean percentage discount Σ((MSRP - Current) / MSRP × 100) / N for all products with reference prices.
    Market Trend Direction Counter showMarketTrendKpi

    Tracks broader price momentum. Compares the average catalog price index over the past 7 days against a 30-day baseline to reveal macro inflation or deflation.

    Calculation: ((7-day average index - 30-day baseline index) / 30-day index) × 100%. Negative values indicate falling/deflationary prices.

    Product Cards (Grid & List)

    4 Metrics · Catalog Browse Views
    Deal Score Badge showDealScoreBadge

    A normalized rating from 0 to 100 displayed on product cards in both grid and list views. Allows rapid scanning and sorting of top-tier deals.

    Calculation: 40% weight on reference price discount depth + 60% weight on historical percentile position (90-day / 365-day range). Scores ≥75 indicate superior deals.
    Price Freshness & Stability Text showPriceFreshness

    Clean textual indicator on product cards showing elapsed time since the merchant last changed the price (e.g., "Changed 2d ago" or "Stable for 33d").

    Calculation: Analyzes timestamps of historical price records. Formatted with clean typography without pill or badge containers.
    Store Price Spread Text showStoreSpread

    Highlights the exact price arbitrage between the cheapest and the most expensive retailer for products with multiple monitored merchant sources.

    Calculation: (highestStorePrice - lowestStorePrice). Displayed as clean comparative text (e.g., "Spread: save 32 vs Maxxess").
    All-Time Low Proximity Text showAtlProximity

    When an item is not currently at its record low, this text reveals how close the current price sits above the all-time low.

    Calculation: (currentPrice - minPriceEver) and percentage distance ((current - min) / min × 100). Formatted as "+5.20 from ATL (+3.2%)".

    Product Detail Page

    5 Metrics · Detailed Product Inspection
    Buy Advice & Deal Score Meter showBuyAdvice

    The primary intelligence banner on the product detail page. Features an interactive score meter, historical percentile breakdown, and actionable recommendation (Strong Buy, Buy, Fair Price, or Wait).

    Calculation: Multi-factor percentile calculation comparing current price against full historical price range, discount depth, and proximity to all-time low.
    Best Day to Buy (Day-of-Week Sweet Spot) showDayOfWeekSweetSpot

    Discovers retailer pricing routines by analyzing which day of the week historically delivers the lowest prices or the most frequent price drops.

    Calculation: Groups historical price points and drop timestamps by day of week (Monday through Sunday) to find the weekday with the lowest mean price.
    Drop Frequency & Cycle Length showDropCycleMetrics

    Calculates how frequently the merchant discounts the item and reports how many days have elapsed since the last price drop.

    Calculation: Mean elapsed days between consecutive price drop events. Helps predict when the next cyclical clearance or promotional drop is expected.
    Moving Average Benchmark (SMA-30 / SMA-90) showMovingAverageBenchmark

    Filters out day-to-day pricing volatility by benchmarking current pricing against 30-day and 90-day Simple Moving Averages.

    Calculation: SMA = (Σ daily closing prices) / N days. Displays current price deviation percentage relative to SMA-30 and SMA-90 baselines.
    Store Source Volatility Badge showStoreVolatility

    Tags each individual merchant row in the product's store list with its pricing behavior pattern (Dynamic Pricing, Regular Cycles, or Static Price).

    Calculation: Evaluates total price change frequency and fluctuation amplitude observed for that merchant on this specific product.

    Stores & Alerts Pages

    3 Metrics · Stores & Alerts Tabs
    Retailer Volatility & Win-Rate Index showStoreVolatilityTable

    Market intelligence table on the Stores page ranking all active retailers by catalog coverage, lowest-price win rate, pricing volatility tier, and total price adjustments.

    Calculation: Win rate = (catalog products where store offers lowest price / total products tracked by store) × 100%, paired with volatility classification.
    Scraper Latency & Health Stats showStoreLatencyHealth

    Diagnostic metric column in the Retailer Volatility table displaying scraper performance, HTTP response speed, and request success rate per merchant.

    Calculation: Mean scraper HTTP fetch time in milliseconds and percentage of successful price extractions (e.g., "780ms · 99.2% ok").
    Alert Target Realism Score showAlertRealismScore

    Feasibility assessment on the dedicated Alert Detail view (#alert/:id). Assesses whether a user's price alert threshold is achievable or overly ambitious based on historical price history.

    Calculation: Compares target price against record low (ATL) and daily price distribution to assign feasibility (High, Moderate, or Optimistic), historical hit rate (days target was met / total days tracked), and distance to target.
